## Content
After working on my freedom project website about Performing Arts, there were many takeaways that I learned when I presented in class. One of them was that it was really important to rehearse what I am going to say and practice at home so that my presentation can go smoothly. Making eye contact with everyone in the class while talking about my project and my slides was really important to also engage the audience. The elevator pitch also came with some takeaways such as knowing what to say in a sort amount of time to let the audience understand what my project is about and include the most important thing which is my future technology inventions as that is what makes my project unique. Changing my tone of voice and being enthusiastic even if the audience isnt makes my project and my presentation be able to engage others instead of giving the same energy back which doesnt help.
